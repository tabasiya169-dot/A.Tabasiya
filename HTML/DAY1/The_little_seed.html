The Little Seed Once, a little seed was lying in the soil. It wanted to become a big tree, but it was afraid of the dark ground. Still, it stayed patient and accepted the rain, sunlight, and wind. Days passed, and the seed slowly grew into a small plant. It faced many storms, but it never gave up. With time, it became a strong tree. Birds built nests on its branches, and people enjoyed its cool shade. The little seed understood that every challenge helped it grow stronger. It was happy because it never stopped believing in itself. Moral: Patience and hard work always bring success.
