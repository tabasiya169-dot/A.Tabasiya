How the Platform Works

A streamlined 4-step pipeline ensuring leftover food is securely listed, safely handled, and quickly delivered.

Step 1

Post Leftover Food

Fill our simple donation form with meal name, category, quantity, preparation time, and exact pickup location.

Step 2

Safety Verification

Our platform checks safety guidelines and temperature windows to guarantee clean, safe food for recipients.

Step 3

Claim & Coordinate

Local shelter volunteers or needy individuals browse listings and request pickup with their contact details.

Step 4

Nourish & Complete

The donor hands over the meal. The donation status updates to Completed, updating community statistics.
